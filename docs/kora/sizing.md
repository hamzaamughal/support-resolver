# Sizing and fit

## General fit
Kora tops and outerwear have a relaxed fit. If you are between sizes or prefer a closer fit, choose the smaller size. Pants and jeans fit true to size.

## Tops size chart (chest, inches)
- XS: 33–35
- S: 35–37
- M: 38–40
- L: 41–43
- XL: 44–46
- XXL: 47–49

## Pants size chart (waist, inches)
- 28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38. Inseam is 32 inches on all pants. We do not offer other inseam lengths.

## Shrinkage
Our cotton tees are pre-washed and shrink less than 3% when washed cold and line dried. Washing hot or tumble drying on high can shrink them up to one size.

## Fabric and care
Tees: 100% organic cotton. Hoodies: 80% organic cotton, 20% recycled polyester. Wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low or line dry. Do not bleach.
