# Returns, exchanges and refunds

## Return window
You can return unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached within 30 days of delivery.

## Items that cannot be returned
Final-sale items (marked "Final sale" on the product page), underwear, socks and gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged.

## How to start a return
Go to kora-apparel.example/returns, enter your order number and email, and choose the items. You will get a prepaid return label by email.

## Return shipping cost
Returns within the United States are free. For Canada and the UK, a flat $9.95 return label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Exchanges
We offer free size exchanges in the United States for the same item in a different size, if it is in stock. Choose "Exchange" when you start your return. For any other change, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days after your return reaches our warehouse. Your bank may take a further 5–10 business days to show it. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item, email a photo within 7 days of delivery. We cover all shipping and send a replacement or full refund.
