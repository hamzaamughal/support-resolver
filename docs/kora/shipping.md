# Shipping

## Where we ship
Kora Apparel ships to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We do not currently ship to other countries. Orders with an address outside these three countries are cancelled and fully refunded automatically.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day are packed and handed to the carrier the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on weekends and US public holidays, ship the next business day.

## Delivery times and costs
- United States, Standard (USPS): 3–6 business days. Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95.
- United States, Express (UPS): 1–2 business days. $18.
- Canada (Canada Post): 6–10 business days. $14.95, free on orders over $150.
- United Kingdom (Royal Mail tracked): 7–12 business days. $16.95, free on orders over $150.

## Customs and duties
For Canada and the UK, duties and import taxes are not included in the price and are collected by the carrier on delivery. Refused parcels are returned to us and refunded minus the original shipping cost.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Changing your address
You can change the shipping address only while the order status is "Processing". Once an order has shipped we cannot redirect it; please contact the carrier with your tracking number.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If tracking has not updated for 7 business days (US) or 14 business days (Canada/UK), contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund, your choice.
