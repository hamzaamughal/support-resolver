# Orders and payments

## Order status meanings
- Processing: we have your order and are packing it. Address changes and cancellations are still possible.
- Shipped: the order is with the carrier. Use the tracking link in your shipping email.
- Delivered: the carrier has marked the order as delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and refunded.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for a full refund while it is still "Processing". Once it has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Shop Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks or cryptocurrency.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed. Discount codes do not apply to gift cards.

## Gift cards
Digital gift cards are delivered by email within 10 minutes and never expire.

## Contact
Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Email support@kora-apparel.example. We reply within one business day.
