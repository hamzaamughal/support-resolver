# Account & Login

## Reset your password
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter your account email and we'll send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam, or wait two minutes and try again — repeated requests invalidate earlier links.

## Change your email address
Open Settings → Account → Email. Enter the new address; we'll send a confirmation link to it. The change takes effect only after you click that link. Your old email stays active until then.

## Two-factor authentication
Beacon supports app-based 2FA (TOTP). Enable it under Settings → Security → Two-factor. Save your backup codes somewhere safe — they are the only way in if you lose your device. We cannot disable 2FA on your behalf without identity verification.

## Can't log in
The most common causes are an unconfirmed email change or an expired reset link. Request a fresh reset link, and make sure you're using the email tied to the account (not an alias).
