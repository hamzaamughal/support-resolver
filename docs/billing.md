# Billing & Refunds

## Plans and pricing
Beacon offers three plans: Free (up to 3 projects), Pro ($12/user/month, unlimited projects), and Business ($24/user/month, adds SSO and priority support). You can change plans anytime from Settings → Billing.

## How refunds work
We offer a full refund within 14 days of any charge, no questions asked. To request a refund, go to Settings → Billing → "Request refund", or reply to your invoice email. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry it three times over six days. Your account stays active during this grace period. Update your card under Settings → Billing → Payment method to resolve it immediately.

## Annual billing
Annual plans are billed once per year and include a 20% discount versus monthly. Switching from monthly to annual applies a prorated credit for the unused portion of your current month.
